# CareMed EMS

Founded by [[David Grayson]] in **Oxford, MS (~2017)** — started with **one ambulance**; grew to **five counties**, nearly **100 employees**, ~**15,000** projected transports/year.

**Sold to AmeriPro Health, LLC in July 2022.** David then served in AmeriPro leadership (VP Revenue Cycle / IT / Strategic Deployment).

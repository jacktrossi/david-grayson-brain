# Estelle’s

**Venue order: 2 of 3** · [[Oxford Square]] · Parent: [[Backwoods Hospitality Group]]  
**Brand kit:** `40 Brands/Estelles-Diner/`  
**Public name:** Estelle’s / Estelle’s Oxford (not “Diner” on the website — discovery used “Estelle’s Diner”; prefer **Estelle’s** in guest copy)

## Public positioning

Restaurant and cocktail bar on Van Buren. Seasonal American with French influences; regional / farm-to-table lean. Dinner + weekend brunch. Named after **Estelle Faulkner**. Building once mailed William Faulkner’s manuscripts.

Opened **June 2025** (Neighborhood Realty / chamber anticipated June 2025).

**Nested concept:** [[1929]] — unmarked-door back bar (own brand; FCFS; no Resy).

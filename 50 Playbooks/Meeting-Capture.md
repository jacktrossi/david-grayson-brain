# Playbook: Meeting Capture

**Trigger:** After a call/meeting; Zoom/Teams notes; “file this meeting”.

## Steps

1. Use `70 Templates/Meeting-Note.md`
2. Save to `80 Journal-Meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-{{slug}}.md`
3. Wiki-link org + people
4. Extract action items with owners/dates
5. If venue brand/ops decisions: also patch org or brand notes

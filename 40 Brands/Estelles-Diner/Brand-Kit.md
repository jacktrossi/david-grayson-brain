# Estelle’s — Brand Kit

Venue 2 of 3 · [[Oxford Square]] · Parent: [[Backwoods Hospitality Group]] · Org: [[Estelle’s]]

**Quality bar:** Fable-5 intentional. Editorial hospitality — not generic “French bistro AI.”

## Positioning (public)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| One-liner | Seasonal Southern / French-influenced restaurant & cocktail bar on Van Buren — dinner, brunch, and a bar worth lingering at |
| Audience | Guests who want a slower downtown Oxford meal; literary-history curious; brunch crowd |
| Occasion | Dinner Tue–Sat; weekend brunch; private events / rehearsal dinners |
| Differentiator | Faulkner/Estelle story + chef-driven seasonal menu; [[1929]] is the unmarked sequel behind the door |

Named for **Estelle Faulkner**. Building once mailed William Faulkner’s manuscripts; beside Faulkner’s Alley. Opened June 2025.

## Brand story (public language to protect)

> “A restaurant and cocktail bar on Van Buren Avenue, in a downtown Oxford building that once mailed William Faulkner’s manuscripts out into the world. Now it’s writing a new chapter.” — estelles-oxford.com

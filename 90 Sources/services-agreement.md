## 1. Scope of Services

Service Provider will design, build, configure, and maintain **GraysonOS**, a custom AI-powered personal intelligence operating system for Client. GraysonOS is built on Claude AI, Supabase, Composio, and various third-party integrations, and includes the following:

- **Email triage** across Client's connected Gmail accounts
- **Calendar management** across Client's connected Google Calendars
- **Client intelligence** and record-keeping
- **Compliance tooling** (e.g., disclaimer handling and review flags)
- **Travel management**
- **A skills library** of reusable automations
- Integrations with Client's email, calendars, Slack, Zoom, and other tools as configured

Service Provider will also provide **ongoing maintenance and updates** to keep GraysonOS running, including hosting management, API maintenance, bug fixes, and reasonable improvements over time.

**Included modifications:** The monthly retainer includes up to **[X] hours** of modifications, additions, or enhancements per month. Unused hours do not roll over to the following month. Work requested beyond the included hours will be quoted and billed separately, and only performed with Client's prior approval.

- **Term:** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. After the initial setup is complete, the engagement continues on a **month-to-month** basis.
- **Termination:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason with **30 days' written notice** to the other Party.
- **Fees owed:** Upon termination, Client owes all outstanding fees through the termination date, including a pro-rated portion of the final month if applicable.
- **Transition on termination:** Upon termination, Service Provider will:
  - Deliver to Client all credentials, API keys, and configuration details necessary for Client to operate or transfer GraysonOS;

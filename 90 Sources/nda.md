---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties are exploring and/or engaged in a technology services relationship involving the design, build, and operation of **GraysonOS** (the "Engagement"). In connection with the Engagement, each Party may share confidential information with the other. This Agreement protects that information.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, in any form (written, oral, electronic, or visual), that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances. It includes, without limitation:

- Technical architecture, system designs, and configurations
- Source code, scripts, and software
- Business processes, methods, and know-how
- Client lists, contacts, and relationships
- Financial information
- Trade secrets

**Exclusions.** Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

- Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party;
- Was already lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure;
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without using the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
- Is rightfully received from a third party without any obligation of confidentiality.

---

## 3. Obligations

Each Receiving Party agrees to:

- **Keep confidential** — hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to anyone except as permitted by this Agreement;
- **Use only for the Engagement** — use the Confidential Information solely to evaluate, perform, or support the Engagement, and for no other purpose;
- **Limit disclosure** — share Confidential Information only with employees, contractors, or advisors who have a legitimate need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement; and
- **Protect it** — safeguard the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than **reasonable care**.

---

## 4. Term

- This Agreement's confidentiality obligations remain in effect for **two (2) years from the Effective Date**.
- **Trade secrets** remain protected for **as long as they qualify as trade secrets** under applicable law, even after the two-year period ends.

---

## 5. Permitted Disclosures

If a Receiving Party is **legally required** to disclose Confidential Information (for example, by court order, subpoena, or regulator), it may do so, provided that — to the extent legally permitted — it:

- Gives the Disclosing Party **prompt written notice** before disclosing, so the Disclosing Party has a chance to seek protection; and
- Discloses only the portion of Confidential Information that is legally required.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property Protection

For clarity, the **GraysonOS source code, system architecture, framework, and methodology** are the confidential and proprietary property of the Service Provider (Jack Rossi). They are Confidential Information under this Agreement, and nothing in this Agreement transfers any ownership of them to the Client. The Client may use GraysonOS only as configured for the Client and under the terms of the separate services agreement between the Parties.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction of Information

Upon the Disclosing Party's request, or upon termination of the Engagement, the Receiving Party will promptly **return or destroy** all Confidential Information in its possession, including copies, and (if requested) confirm in writing that it has done so. The Receiving Party may retain copies required by law or routine backup systems, which remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

---

## 8. Remedies

The Parties agree that a breach of this Agreement may cause **irreparable harm** for which money damages alone would not be an adequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek **injunctive relief** to prevent or stop a breach, **without the need to post a bond**, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

---

## 9. General

- **No license:** This Agreement does not grant either Party any license or ownership rights in the other's Confidential Information, except the limited right to use it for the Engagement.
- **No obligation to proceed:** Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into or continue the Engagement.
- **Entire agreement:** This Agreement is the entire understanding between the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes any prior confidentiality discussions.
- **Amendments:** Any changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
- **Severability:** If any provision is found unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement remains in effect.

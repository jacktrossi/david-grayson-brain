# Skill: Interview — Brand Venue

**Trigger:** “interview brand”, “interview Whiskey Ranch brand”, etc.

**Purpose:** Fill one venue brand kit to Fable-5 readiness (tokens may still need asset files).

## Venue order

If “all brands” or unspecified next: **Whiskey Ranch → Estelle’s Diner → 1929** (one venue per session unless David wants to continue).

## Read first

`40 Brands/[Venue]/Brand-Kit.md` — only ask for empty / `needs-david` fields.

## Question arc (one at a time)

1. One-liner — what is this place in one sentence?
2. Who is the guest? What occasion?
3. How is it different from the other two on the Square?
4. Colors you already use (hex if known) / must-never colors
5. Fonts / logo files — where do they live? (point to Assets drop)
6. Voice: three adjectives; a sample IG caption; what would sound wrong
7. Photography mood and bans
8. “What must never happen” for this brand

## Writes to

`40 Brands/[Venue]/Brand-Kit.md` (+ Assets README notes)

Use Interview-Capture template for session log if useful.

## Quality

Push for specificity. Reject vague “fun and friendly” without examples. Remind Fable-5 bar: intentional, sparse, premium — not generic restaurant AI.

# Skill: Brandkit (Fable-5 Bar)

**Trigger:** Generate or refine brand boards, identity decks, visual-world presentations for BHG or a venue.

## Directive

Act as an elite brand identity art director. Output must feel intentional, premium, minimal, coherent, visually expensive — **as if Fable 5 designed it**.

## Do

- One strong brand idea per board
- Sparse typography, large negative space
- Real token systems (color, type, lockups) grounded in `40 Brands/[Venue]/`
- Clear application mockups (menu, social, signage) when useful

## Don’t

- Generic logos or random mockup soup
- Purple-gradient restaurant AI defaults
- Fake script fonts as the entire identity
- Inventing hex/logo when kit says `needs-david` — mark provisional or interview first

## Read first

Venue or parent brand kit. For net-new exploration, label outputs **exploratory** until David confirms.
